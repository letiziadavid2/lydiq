Why LYDIQ

Don't just forecast cash.
Stress-test the decision.

Most small businesses discover pressure when the problem is already visible in the bank account. LYDIQ is designed around the earlier question: what is changing, why, and what happens if I act?

01 / Detect

Spot pressure earlier

Combine a few core financial signals into a simple, explainable view of liquidity, working-capital and leverage pressure.

02 / Explain

Know what is driving it

Turn a score into the underlying drivers, not a black-box number that leaves the manager guessing.

03 / Decide

See the effect of action

Simulate growth, slower collections or new debt and compare the financial consequences before committing.

The workflow

Pressure → Explanation → Decision → Action

Start with a lightweight assessment today. The long-term product can connect richer data sources later — but the decision workflow should stay simple.

Pilot

Validate the problem with real operators.

The first version should be tested with 5–10 people who manage, advise or analyse SMEs. The goal is not compliments; it is evidence that the workflow solves a real decision problem.

Ask

“How do you spot pressure today?”

Understand the current process before showing the product.

Test

Give them one decision.

Ask them to simulate collections, growth or debt and observe where they hesitate.

Measure

Would they use it again?

Track repeat intent, the problem they valued most, and what data they would actually provide.

From a good idea to evidence.

LYDIQ is currently a product prototype. The next milestone is simple: put it in front of real SME operators, learn what matters, and build only what the evidence supports.
